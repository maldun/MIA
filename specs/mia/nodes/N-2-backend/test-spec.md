# Test specification: Backend (N-2)

**Requirements:** specs/mia/nodes/N-2-backend/requirements.md   **Status:** Draft (Plan for node N-2, 2026-10-26)

The tests of the inner node: the parts put together step by step in the order of `decomposition.yaml` (N-2.5 → N-2.3 → N-2.4 → N-2.2 → N-2.1); step 0 is the skeleton with all parts as mocks. Each step case decides the requirements whose last refining part is integrated at that step. The tests of the parts are in their own test specifications. Model-dependent criteria are decided by the eval suites on the pinned model, never by these cases. Stages (M27): 1 = commit gate, 2 = up to 10 minutes, 3 = up to 30 minutes, 4 = eval runs and measurements.
