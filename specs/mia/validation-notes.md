# Notes for the validation phase: points to review once there is field data

Collected during the plan gate (2026-10-26). Nothing here changes a requirement now: the owner accepts the risk and studies the points with real use (phase 7, VAL-006). Each note says what is unspecified, why it matters and what the agent would propose. Close a note by an amendment of the named requirements and tests, or by writing "kept" with the reason.
